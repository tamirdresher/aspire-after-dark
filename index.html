Your stack,

Aspire gives you a code-first way to model, run and deploy distributed apps. Describe your services, databases and integrations in one AppHost, and Aspire takes care of orchestration, service discovery and telemetry.

Get started ▶ Watch the demo
$ aspire new aspire-starter
AppHost.cs
var builder = DistributedApplication.CreateBuilder(args);

var cache = builder.AddRedis("cache");
var db = builder.AddPostgres("pg")
               .AddDatabase("catalog");

var api = builder.AddProject<Projects.Api>("api")
                 .WithReference(cache)
                 .WithReference(db);

builder.AddViteApp("web", "../web")
       .WithReference(api);

builder.Build().Run();
